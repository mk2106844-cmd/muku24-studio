Free Joint Photo Maker – Two People in One Photo

Joint Photo Maker by Smart CSC Tools lets you make a couple photo, family or friends photo and joint passport-style photo online, for free. Upload one photo that already has both people, or upload two separate photos and join them in a single picture with a clean background of your choice. It is handy for CSC centres, cyber cafés and photo studios that need quick joint photos and print sheets.

How to make a joint photo

  1. Pick a section: “Both people in one photo” or “Two separate photos”.
  2. Crop: choose the joint photo size (for example 7 × 4.5 cm) and crop. Auto Crop can detect faces.
  3. Edit: remove the background with AI, choose a colour or gradient, align both people and correct the colour.
  4. Print: choose copies and paper (A4, A5, A3, Letter, 4×6 in and more), then print directly or download a PDF / JPG.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The tool runs in your browser. Photos and the AI model stay on your device.

Can I join two separate photos into one?

Yes. Use the “Two separate photos” section. Each person is cut out by AI, then you can auto-align faces, resize, move, rotate and flip each person and match their colours.

Which background colours can I use?

Any colour using the colour picker, ready-made swatches (white, blue, red, grey and more) and gradients. In the single-photo section you can also keep the original background.

How do I print many copies on one sheet?

In the Print step set the number of copies and the paper size. The tool arranges as many photos as fit on each sheet, adds more sheets automatically, and lets you print or download a PDF or JPG.

The background removal missed a part. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open “Refine Edges” and use the Erase and Restore brushes to correct any area.
