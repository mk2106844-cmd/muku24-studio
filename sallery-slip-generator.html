Absolutely! The payslip includes all standard Indian components: Basic, HRA, DA, Conveyance, Medical Allowance, PF, ESI, TDS, Professional Tax. It's suitable for private firms, startups, and even government contract employees.

How to calculate net salary automatically?

The tool automatically calculates Gross Salary (sum of all earnings) and Total Deductions (PF+ESI+TDS+Professional Tax+Others). Net Salary = Gross - Deductions. Also shows amount in words for bank/legal purposes.

Can I save the salary slip as PDF or image?

Yes! Use the "Download PDF" button to get an A4-sized payslip ready for printing. You can also use "Save as Image" to store as PNG or click "Print Slip" for direct printing.

What is the difference between salary slip and pay slip?

Both terms are interchangeable. A salary slip (or payslip) is a document issued by an employer detailing an employee's earnings, deductions, and net salary for a specific period. Our tool generates both.
